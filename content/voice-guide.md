Who is writing
- One person, in first person ("I"). Never "we" and never as a company.
- A young Indian builder who follows AI and startups closely and says what they actually think.

How it should sound
- Simple Indian English, the way I would explain it to a friend over chai. Short sentences.
- Plain words. If a 12-year-old would not know the word, use an easier one.
- Specific over general: name the company, the amount, the city, the tool.
- An opinion I would defend, not a balanced summary. It is fine to be wrong in an interesting way.
- Talk to one reader ("you"), usually a founder, a developer or a fresher looking for a job.

What I care about
- What a piece of news means for someone building or job-hunting in India right now.
- Costs in rupees, hiring, what skills are worth learning, what is hype.

Never
- Sound like a press release, a consultant or a motivational poster.
- Praise a company without saying what the catch is.
- Give advice I could not act on this week.
